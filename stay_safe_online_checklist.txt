Stay Safe Online Checklist (For College Students in India)

1. Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts (mix of letters, numbers, symbols).
2. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on important apps like email, banking, and social media.
3. Never share your OTP, PIN, or passwords with anyone—even if they claim to be from a bank.
4. Do not click on suspicious links received through SMS, email, or social media.
5. Always verify the sender before responding to unknown messages or calls.
6. Keep your apps and devices updated to avoid security vulnerabilities.
7. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for logging into banking or sensitive accounts.
8. Be cautious while sharing personal information online (address, phone number, ID details).

UPI / Financial Safety Tips:
1. Never scan QR codes to receive money—scanning is only for sending money.
2. Double-check UPI IDs and payment details before transferring money.
3. Use official banking apps only—avoid third-party or unknown apps.
4. Immediately report any unauthorized transaction to your bank.

Reporting Cybercrime:
If you become a victim of cybercrime, report immediately:
- National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Helpline Number: 1930 (24x7)
